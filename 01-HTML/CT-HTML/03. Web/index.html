Bhopal - City Of Lake

Bhopal is the capital city of the Indian state of Madhya Pradesh. It is known as the “City of Lakes” because of its beautiful natural and artificial lakes.

Delhi - Capital Of India

Delhi is the capital city of India and one of the oldest cities in the world, blending ancient history with modern development. It has served as the seat of power f heritage, bustling markets, diverse cuisine, and vibrant festivals
